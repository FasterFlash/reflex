What got me here.

I paid for YouTube Premium for months and couldn’t point to one thing that got better. So I built the thing I wished it had.

  • The itchPremium, with nothing to show.

  • The ideaA Wrapped for YouTube. Deeper.

    It’s coming to the analysis soon, for music only. Stay tuned.
