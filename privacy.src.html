Ok but what do you do with my file?

Fair question. Short version: I read it once, make your report, and let it go. Long version below, including the parts that don’t make me look great.

  1. 01 · What I open

    Only the YouTube files you give me.

    Watch history, search history, subscriptions. Nothing else from Google, since you only ticked YouTube in step 3. Anything else in the export, like a kids profile, gets ignored.

  2. 02 · Where it lives

    In memory while I work. Not in a database.

    Your file gets read, counted and dropped. Your history never goes into a database or a log.

  3. 03 · About AI

    AI never reads your history. Only a tiny summary, and only if you tap it.

    The tagline button sends Google’s Gemini about 20 numbers: your hours, the videos vs Shorts split, your top three categories, your busiest time of day, your longest sitting and Shorts scroll, and your top 5 channel names. Never your name, a video title, a search, a timestamp or the file. The report shows you the exact payload before anything leaves, and the tile works fine without it.

    Why: so the taglines can say something real about you. The catch: this runs on Gemini’s free tier. Google’s terms for that tier say it may use what it receives to improve its products, and people there may read it. It doesn’t get your name, but a list of channels is still a bit of a fingerprint. That’s why it’s opt-in, and why there’s no chatbot: nobody can type anything to the model.

  4. 04 · When it’s gone

    Report’s done, file’s gone.

    I keep no copy of your ZIP. Close the report and nothing about you is left on my side.

  5. 05 · Don’t just trust me

    The code that reads it is public.

    See exactly what happens to your file, line by line, instead of trusting a paragraph on a website.

What I read

  • Video titles + watch times. To count hours and spot your habits.
  • Channel names. To see who you’re actually watching.
  • Your searches. To catch what you keep looking up.
  • Your subscriptions. To compare who you follow with who you watch.

What I never do

  • Sell it or hand it to advertisers.
  • Train a model on your history. The AI taglines only get the summary above.
  • Keep a copy of your file.
  • Make you sign up or ask for your email.

The honest part

What I can’t promise, tbh.

  • Your file does travel. It goes from your browser to a server over an encrypted connection, and that server holds it for the minute or so it takes to process.
  • Video IDs go to YouTube. To get each video’s length and category, I ask YouTube about the IDs you watched. Not when, not how often, not who you are. Those public facts get cached so the next lookup’s faster.
  • You’re trusting me while it runs. The public code lets you check what I say, but it can’t force the server to run exactly that code.
  • I’m one person. Solo project, not a company with a security team and an audit.
  • The AI part runs on a free tier. It’s a student project, so I use Gemini’s free tier, where Google may keep and learn from what it gets. If I move to a paid tier I’ll change this page.
  • Feedback is the one thing I keep. If you use the feedback form, I store your message, its type, the time, and a reply address only if you type one. Never your IP, never anything from your report.
  • Your ZIP still sits with Google. Once your report’s done, you can delete that download yourself.
