Four steps. That’s it.

About two minutes of clicking, then a bit of waiting.

  1. 1

    Open your Google account

    Tap your profile pic, then Manage your Google Account.

    Clip · step 1
  2. 2

    Find your data

    Hit Data & privacy, scroll down, pick Download my data.

    Clip · step 2
  3. 3

    Pick only YouTube

    Deselect all, tick YouTube only, set history to JSON, then hit Request.

    Clip · step 3
  4. 4

    Check your email

    Google emails you a link, usually in 1–4 hours. Grab the ZIP and come back.

    Clip · step 4

Worth knowing

I can only read what Google kept. If your history auto-deletes, your report starts from the oldest day that’s left, and I’ll say so. Cleared or paused history leaves gaps too.
