So what even am I.

I read your YouTube history back to you. Give me one file and I’ll tell you what your watching says about you. Brace a little.

You have

A file Google sat on.

Every video you watch gets logged. Google lets you download it. It just never brings it up.

I do

The reading.

I count, sort and spot patterns: when you watch, who you watch, what you keep going back to.

You get

A report to flex.

Numbers worth screenshotting, plus a few lines that are annoyingly accurate.

What you get.

  • 01
    Hours watched

    Each play counts until your next one starts, never longer than the video. Where I can't tell, you get a range.

  • 02
    Videos vs Shorts

    Up to 60 s is a Short. 1-3 min counts as one if it says #shorts or sits between Shorts. The rest are videos.

  • 03
    Your top channels

    Your plays grouped by channel, ranked two ways: by time watched and by number of plays.

  • 04
    Your top categories

    The category YouTube gives each video, ranked by how much time you spent in it.

  • 05
    Your top searches

    Your searches matched on the exact words, then counted. Repeats are the ones that rise.

  • 06
    The subs you ghost

    Your subscription list against the channels you actually watched in this window.

  • 07
    Every day, on a calendar

    Hours per day, counted on the day each watch started, in Indian time.

  • 08
    Your peak hours

    Time added up per weekday and hour across the whole period. Darker means more.

  • 09
    Your longest binge

    A sitting ends after 30 minutes of nothing. The longest one wins, and so does the longest Shorts scroll.

What I’m not

No app, no extension, no login. I don’t want your Google password, ever. You give me a file, not your account.
